and artifact-check005. No decipherment or word meanings established. This design

Do not use the ordinary word parser to infer glyphs: it intentionally excludes
rare codes and lowercases basic text. Preserve case, high-ASCII escapes,
uncertainty brackets and compounds. An @169;v group is NOT seven ordinary
letters. IVTFF dot/comma delimiters describe apparent spacing, not semantic
character boundaries. A17-position sequence is not a17-distinct-letter alphabet.

RF is a combination of ZL and GC and must not count as independent evidence.

variation. The f/f/p/p slot is an old observation, not a novel finding. It is
consistent with a common template role but does not prove allography. The
four-cycle order may encode a state change rather than spelling variation.
